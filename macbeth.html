Act I: The Prophecy

Location: A Foggy Heath near Forres

Returning from victorious battle, Macbeth and Banquo encounter three mysterious Witches. The sisters prophecy that Macbeth will become Thane of Cawdor and then King, while Banquo's descendants will inherit the throne. As the witches vanish, messengers arrive confirming Macbeth is indeed the new Thane of Cawdor. Ambition takes root in his heart.

"Fair is foul, and foul is fair: Hover through the fog and filthy air."

— The Witches, Act I, Scene I
